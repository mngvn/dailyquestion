🔦 Rare Air

You're cave diving. Every junction is a question, and your answer picks the tunnel. Give the answer everyone gives and you follow the crowd down a short tunnel where everyone is breathing the same stale oxygen. Give a rare one and you find a tunnel nobody has breathed in: fresh air, and it goes deep.

Every junction uses oxygen, and you breathe faster the deeper you go. You'll see the divers who gave worse answers turn back in shallower tunnels. An answer that isn't on the map is a dead end: the tunnel caves in, you lose oxygen, and you try again.

Everyone gets the same cave each day. Free dives are random.
